Implemented source
- Objective/NLRitualObjective: independent Integrity resource, 0–100 rescue progress, default 240 effective combat seconds.
- Transient stable-ID interference sources, death/destruction cleanup, explicit interruption/removal.
- Separate required-threat registration. An owner may also register already flying ANLCombatProjectile instances; reflected/player shots cease to count.
- CompletionPending is announced at 100%; final completion waits until a later game frame and no required living threats. Zero integrity on the 100%-candidate frame wins failure regardless of progress/damage call order.
- Failure/completion events fire once per encounter. Restoring a terminal snapshot does not replay reward events.
- World pause and local SetCombatPaused freeze progress; the resolver's objective gate rejects local paused/restoring/inactive/terminal targets.
- AllowedAttackDefinitionIds is an explicit enemy attack whitelist. Player friendly fire is rejected centrally by the combat resolver.
- Ritual damage uses the common vitals/resolver pipeline, its own 0-second damage protection, Outcome RitualDamaged and Combat.RitualDamaged feedback.

Snapshot contract
CaptureObjectiveSnapshot returns the already supported FNLObjectiveSnapshot schema. No arbitrary actor references or projectiles are serialized.
RestoreObjectiveSnapshot first validates objective ID, finite bounds, terminal consistency and unique stable source IDs; an invalid restore leaves the objective unchanged.
For a snapshot with interference IDs, progress and damage remain frozen until the wave owner rebinds reconstructed live actors with RegisterInterferenceSource and calls FinishSourceRebuild. FinishSourceRebuild returns the count of absent IDs explicitly discarded. Missing/dead actors never become permanent ghost interference.
Required threats are transient encounter state and must be reconstructed by the encounter owner, not restored as arbitrary projectile state.
