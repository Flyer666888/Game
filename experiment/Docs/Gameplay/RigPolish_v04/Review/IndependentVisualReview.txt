V04 authoring and acceptance constraints
- Author the arm and grip silhouette first; derive the sword path from it. Do not solve a fixed sword tip target by folding or rolling the wrist.
- Start from the reference wrist relationship, then make small adjustments verified against the actual skinned closed hand and attached sword. A numerical distance to Idle is not a pass criterion.
- Guard: keep the right elbow low and outside the right ribs, the grip clear of chest/face, and the forearm-to-hand outline continuous in front and side views.
- Strike: shoulder and elbow move the grip through the right-front region; the sword follows the grip. Avoid a sudden independent hand flip between contact frames. Do not let the arm cross through the chest to reach a fixed endpoint.
- Recovery: allow the grip to settle in a clear right-side lane before returning to guard. Do not pull the hilt through the other hand or abdomen merely to reach the old end pose quickly.
- Left hand: relaxed and visibly separate from the hilt, wrist, and blade. Do not hold an unrelated world orientation while its elbow changes.
- Inspect front, side, and three-quarter views at .60, .80, .90, 1.03, 1.30, plus the full motion. Acceptance requires an intelligible continuous grip/arm silhouette, no visible wrist kink, no hand-through-sleeve or forearm-through-body appearance, and no abrupt roll reversal.
- Collision and motion metrics are secondary checks. Any obvious broken silhouette or implausible wrist pose vetoes acceptance even when all metrics improve.

Hands mesh candidate A: GRIP MORPH REJECTED
Inspected Docs/Gameplay/RigPolish_v04/Audit/NaturalHands/Hand_0.90_Front/Side.png and Hand_1.03_Front/Side.png.
The side exposing the palm shows the hilt resting on hooked fingertips, with the broad palm still open and the thumb floating above instead of opposing the fingers. This is a grasp-shape failure, not merely low-poly styling. The front view's sword guard hides the problem and must not be used as the only acceptance angle.
Required revision: bend the proximal finger segments inward, establish contact between palm and back of hilt, and oppose the thumb toward the index/middle side. Curling the fingertips further or only moving the thumb is insufficient. Preserve the approved wrist trajectory and attachment basis while correcting the morph.
Current overall verdict: joint motion accepted; grip candidate A rejected; awaiting candidate B palm-side evidence.

Replacement-hand review boundary
The work has moved to a closed-grip replacement hand derived from the project's existing player-hand source. The original hand's fixed shaft position was an implementation assumption, not a user requirement. Preserving it caused the replacement wrist to be stretched unnaturally. That constraint is explicitly withdrawn: the hand may be placed rigidly at a natural wrist join, with the sword's hand-local grip transform moved consistently. Do not stretch the palm or wrist to preserve the obsolete socket offset. Final judgment will use the natural wrist join and closed-grip appearance; the parent will independently revalidate actual hit reach with the new transform.
